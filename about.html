About Our Bakery

Our Story

Neighborhood Hearth Bakery started with a simple goal: to provide fresh, handmade baked goods to the local community. We enjoy serving families, commuters, businesses, and anyone looking for something freshly baked.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe good baked goods start with good ingredients. We use quality flour, fresh dairy products, seasonal fruit, and locally available ingredients whenever possible.
